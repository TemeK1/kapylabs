# Havu Apps brand assets

The evergreen sprig is the current Havu Apps mark.

- `havu-logo.png`: transparent full wordmark, 1080 × 320.
- `havu-mark-1024.png`: transparent square sprig mark. Also available at 256 and 512 pixels.
- `-light` variants: off-white for dark backgrounds.
- `-dark` variants: dark forest green for light backgrounds.
- Matching SVG files remain editable.

Older `pinefall-*` exports are retained as archived artwork. Use `havu-*` for current branding.
